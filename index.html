What a night with live music should do for you

You want guests to stay for one more round and come back next week. That's the job.

Keep people at the table
Music sets the mood without taking over the room. I read the crowd and adjust volume and song choice as the night goes.
Songs your crowd already knows
John Denver and Tom Petty to Chris Stapleton, Oasis and Coldplay. The stuff people sing along to without being asked. I'll tailor the set to your regulars.
Nothing for your staff to manage
I bring my own sound system, set up in a small footprint, and arrive early. All you need is a spot and a power outlet.
Fill the nights that need it
A slow weeknight, a patio weekend, a release party or a holiday event. Tell me what you're trying to build and we'll plan around it.

Your guests pick the songs

Requests run through Bribe The Band, an app I built for exactly this. Nobody shouts over the bar, and there's nothing for your staff to set up.

  1. Guests scan the QR code on my stand.
  2. They pick a song from my list on their phone, and add a tip if they want to.
  3. It comes straight to me, and they stick around to hear it.
